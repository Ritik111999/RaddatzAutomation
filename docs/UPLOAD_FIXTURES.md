# Upload Fixtures

The automation uses two fictional local files for upload coverage:

- `property-inspection-memorandum.docx` - a one-page exterior-inspection memorandum with no client, tenant, case, or account data.
- `property-exterior-inspection.png` - a generated exterior property image with no readable address, people, logo, or identifying signage.

They are used only after the scenario creates its own Matter and Event. They must never be uploaded to existing Companies, Properties, Matters, Tasks, Events, or client records.
